About Ray Choice Center

Our Journey of Trust & Service

For over a decade, Ray Choice Center has been the cornerstone of digital and government services in Civil Line, Lucknow. Founded and led by Aditya Patil, our center has built an unwavering reputation for reliability, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

We started with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between citizens and government services, making essential documents and schemes accessible to everyone. Today, we proudly serve thousands of families across Lucknow and surrounding areas.
